### Open runs and games (backend later)

- Every hour shows who's going and either a scheduled game or an **open
  run**. Joining an open run says "I want to play"; "I'm going" stays
  separate (just showing up to shoot around).
- Games run on the half hour. Notify 10 minutes before and 10 minutes after.
  Teams lock and auto-balance by ELO at game time. Pickleball: 2 → 1v1,
  4 → 2v2. Basketball: even counts up to 5v5. Odd count or not filled: the
  game cancels quietly; optionally notify ~5 minutes before ("invite
  someone"), and let them log a game afterwards.
- Players land on the existing two-sides game screen (`app/run/[id].tsx`).
- Joining an open run must NOT open "schedule a game" (this branch did that;
  he called it illogical; it's removed in `e041eb4`).
- Look at how Street FC does it (verify, don't guess): hosted 1-hour
  sessions, 3 teams of 5, winner stays; peer skill ratings after each game
  (48-hour window) plus a "chill" rating; red cards for no-shows/late
  cancels. He wants their rating/matchmaking studied for ELO later.
